Functions & notation
Up to 8 curves, one per line. Multiplication: 2x or 2*x. Powers: x^2. Constants: pi, e. Angles use radians.
sin, cos, tan, sec, csc, cot, asin, acos, atan, atan2(y,x), sinh, cosh, tanh, asinh, acosh, atanh, sqrt, cbrt, abs, exp, ln, log (base 10), log2, floor, ceil, round, sign, min, max, pow, hypot.
Piecewise: if(x<0, -x, x^2). Comparisons: <, >, <=, >=, ==, !=. Remainder: %.

  
Numerical 2D plots. Fine detail, discontinuities and isolated points may need a narrower range; this is not a symbolic solver.
